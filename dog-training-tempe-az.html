Dog Training in Tempe, AZ

Board & Train and private owner lessons for Tempe dog owners — veteran-owned training built for busy, urban, on-the-go lives.

See Programs & Pricing Book a Call

Tempe is dense, walkable, and always moving — ASU, apartment and condo living, dog-friendly patios along Mill Avenue, and busy paths around Tempe Town Lake. That's a lot of stimulation for a dog. The dogs who thrive here have real leash manners and stay calm and focused in public, around strangers, bikes, scooters, and other dogs. Rogue Intelligence Dog Training is a veteran-owned trainer in nearby Chandler, and public-access polish is exactly what our programs are built to deliver.

Training for a busy, urban dog

We train on three principles — consistency, leadership, and understanding — so your dog can hold it together in the environments that matter, not just in a quiet backyard. Our Public Access programs specifically build the distraction desensitization and social confidence apartment and city dogs need.

  • Board & Train — full-immersion, live-in training 5–6 days a week with video updates, then hands-on owner training to lock it in.
  • Owner Lessons — one-on-one sessions where you learn to handle your dog in real public settings.

Common goals for Tempe dog owners

  • Loose-leash walking on crowded sidewalks and shared paths
  • Calm behavior in apartments, elevators, and dog-friendly patios
  • Neutrality around strangers, bikes, scooters, and other dogs
  • Off-leash reliability and emergency recall for open areas
