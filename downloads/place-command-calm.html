FINDING CALM: THE 'PLACE' COMMAND

The Ultimate Off-Switch for High-Energy Dogs
Does your dog pace the house constantly? Jump on guests when they walk through the door? Beg at the dinner table? The solution to all of these problems is a single, foundational command: Place.

What is 'Place'?

"Place" means your dog must go to a specific boundary (like an elevated cot or a dog bed) and stay there until released. They can stand, sit, or lie down, but all four paws must remain on the boundary.

Why It Works

State of Mind Over Position
When you tell a dog to "Sit," you are commanding a physical posture. It requires intense mental energy for a dog to hold a strict sit-stay. When you tell a dog to "Place," you are commanding a state of mind. You are giving them a job, and that job is simply to relax on their bed. Over time, the physical boundary of the bed triggers a neurological calm down.

How to Use It

Real World Application
When the doorbell rings, instead of fighting your dog to stop jumping, you send them to "Place." They get to watch the guests arrive safely from their bed without practicing bad behavior. It removes conflict and replaces chaos with clarity.
