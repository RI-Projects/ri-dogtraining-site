THE COMPLETE FAQ GUIDE

Answers to Your Most Important Dog Training Questions
Where is Rogue Intelligence Dog Training located?
We're proudly based in Chandler, Arizona. We actively serve the greater Phoenix Valley, which includes our neighbors in Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa, and Phoenix.
How long does the Board & Train program actually take?
The timeline completely depends on your goals. Basic Obedience generally takes 2-3 weeks, Public Access requires 4-5 weeks, and Advanced Obedience runs 6-8 weeks. Keep in mind, every dog is an individual and progresses at their own distinct pace within our structure.
What's the difference between Board & Train and Owner Lessons?
With Board & Train, your dog lives and trains with us daily in a highly structured environment, and then we teach you exactly how to maintain their new behavior at home. Owner Lessons are one-on-one sessions where you and your dog learn and train together as a team from day one.
What is your training philosophy?
Our foundation is built on three pillars: Consistency, Leadership, and Understanding. We train for mutual confidence, clear communication, and teamwork—not just having a dog blindly memorize commands. We want your dog to be able to think, adapt, and stay focused even through real-world distractions.
Do you train puppies or adult dogs?
Both! Our programs are entirely built around your dog's individual needs. Whether that requires foundational shaping work with an 8-week-old puppy or addressing deeply established behavioral issues in a senior dog, we can help.
How much does dog training cost?
Your investment depends on the program format, length, your ultimate goals, and the level of support required. We publish our current live package prices directly on our website so you always have immediate, transparent access to our active catalog.
Are you a veteran-owned business?
Yes. Rogue Intelligence Dog Training is proudly veteran-owned and operated. The very same discipline, structure, and follow-through we lived by in the military carry directly into how we train dogs and coach their handlers.
Do you train reactive, anxious, or aggressive dogs?
Absolutely. We specialize in building programs around each dog's specific behavioral challenges, including intense leash reactivity and severe anxiety. By focusing heavily on building confidence, impulse control, and establishing clear communication, we help your dog stay calm and focused.
Do you offer e-collar training?
Yes, e-collar training is available as an option on select programs. We introduce all tools fairly and clearly, always paired with a solid foundation of obedience and extensive handler education—never as a shortcut around true understanding.
How do progress updates work during Board & Train?
While your dog trains with us 5-6 days a week, we send you consistent video progress updates so you can literally watch the transformation happen in real-time. Every program finishes with hands-on owner training so you are fully equipped to maintain those results at home.
What is the Assistant Trainer Program?
The Assistant Trainer Program delivers the exact same Basic or Advanced Obedience curriculum, but it is delivered by one of our highly skilled Assistant Trainers under Thomas's direct supervision. Every session plan and progress check is reviewed by the lead trainer. You get the same daily structure and same finished dog, at a more accessible investment.
