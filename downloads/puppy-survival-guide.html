THE PUPPY SURVIVAL GUIDE

The First 30 Days: Preventing Bad Habits Before They Start
Bringing a new puppy home is exciting, but the reality of sharp teeth, constant potty breaks, and destroyed shoes sets in quickly. Most owners wait until their dog is 6 months old and completely out of control before they seek training. Don't wait. Training starts the second your puppy walks through the door.

1. The Power of the Crate

Stop Feeling Guilty About Crating
A crate is not a cage; it is a den. Puppies naturally seek small, secure spaces to sleep. Your puppy needs 18-20 hours of sleep per day. Without forced naps in a crate, they become overtired, which leads directly to biting and destruction.

2. Potty Training Protocol

The 100% Supervision Rule
If your puppy is not in their crate, they must be supervised with 100% of your attention. If you cannot watch them, they go in the crate. When they wake up, they go outside. When they finish eating, they go outside. If they have an accident in the house, it is your fault for not watching them.

3. Puppy Biting & Nipping

Redirection & Boundaries
Puppies explore the world with their mouths. Yelling "No!" often just gets them more excited. Instead, consistently redirect their teeth to a designated chew toy. If they persist in biting your hands or clothes, calmly stand up, ignore them, or place them in their crate for a nap. They will quickly learn that biting ends the fun.
