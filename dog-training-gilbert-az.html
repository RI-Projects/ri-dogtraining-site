Dog Training in Gilbert, AZ

Board & Train and one-on-one owner lessons for Gilbert families — veteran-owned, results-driven, and built around the way your household actually lives.

See Programs & Pricing Book a Call

Gilbert is one of the fastest-growing family communities in the Phoenix Valley, and dogs here live busy lives — kids, weekend visitors, trips to Cosmo Dog Park, and walks through master-planned neighborhoods from Agritopia to Power Ranch to Val Vista Lakes. A dog that pulls on the leash, jumps on guests, or bolts for the front door makes all of that harder than it needs to be. Rogue Intelligence Dog Training is a veteran-owned trainer based in nearby Chandler, minutes from Gilbert, and we build dogs that fit into a full, busy family life.

How we train Gilbert dogs

Our programs are built on three principles — consistency, leadership, and understanding. We don't drill memorized commands and hope they hold up; we build dogs that can think, settle, and stay focused through real distractions: doorbells, kids, other dogs, and the chaos of a busy home.

  • Board & Train — your dog lives and trains with us 5–6 days a week, then we teach you how to maintain it at home. Programs run 2–8 weeks with video progress updates throughout.
  • Owner Lessons — you and your dog train together from day one, so you build genuine handler skills, not just a temporarily obedient dog.

Common goals for Gilbert families

  • Loose-leash walking around the neighborhood and at the dog park
  • Calm, polite greetings with kids and visitors instead of jumping
  • A rock-solid recall and a reliable "place"/"stay" for a busy household
  • Confidence work for reactive, anxious, or newly adopted dogs

Puppy or adult, any breed — every plan is built around your dog. See full programs and pricing, or read our guide on Board & Train vs. Owner Lessons to decide which fits.
