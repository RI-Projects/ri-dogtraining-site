Why Does My Puppy Keep Biting Me?

Understanding puppy teething, arousal, and how to stop the needle-teeth biting before it becomes a real behavioral issue.

See Programs & Pricing Back to Guides

Puppy biting is one of the most common complaints we get from new owners in Chandler. It's frustrating, it hurts, and it can make you feel like you adopted a tiny shark instead of a dog.

Why Do They Do It?

Puppies explore the world with their mouths. Between 8 and 16 weeks, they are teething, and biting is a natural soothing mechanism. More importantly, biting is how they play with their littermates. If they bite too hard, the other puppy yelps and stops playing. They are trying to figure out what the boundaries are with you.

How to Stop It

  • Stop pulling away fast: When you jerk your hands away, you turn into a human tug-toy. It makes you fun to bite.
  • Redirect to a toy: Always have a chew toy nearby. When they go for your hands, seamlessly slide the toy into their mouth instead.
  • Enforced Naps: 90% of severe puppy biting happens when the puppy is overtired. A puppy needs 18-20 hours of sleep a day. If they turn into a demon, put them in their crate for a nap.

Consistency is key. If you are struggling, reach out to us and we can help you set up a structure that works.
