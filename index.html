Meet Your Trainers

A Husband-and-Wife Team. 8+ Years. 300+ Dogs Trained.

Rogue Intelligence Dog Training is run by Thomas (Jace) and Keeley — a husband-and-wife team who actually met because of a dog. Jace, a U.S. military veteran, was training Keeley's own dog when the two of them hit it off, and working together turned into a life built entirely around dogs.

Jace brings 8+ years and 300+ dogs of hands-on experience — from family pets that need real-world manners to personal protection and security dogs built to perform reliably under pressure, across working breeds like Belgian Malinois, Dutch Shepherds, German Shepherds, Rottweilers, Doberman Pinschers, Cane Corsos, American Pit Bull Terriers, Mastiffs, and sporting and herding breeds, alongside family dogs of every size and temperament. Keeley co-leads every program too — training and day-to-day care — so your dog gets steady, attentive hands on it the whole way through, not just a name on an invoice.

That partnership is what the training runs on — consistency, leadership, and understanding, from two people who show up for your dog every single day.

Common Questions

FAQ

Everything owners ask before they book — programs, timelines, cost, and how we work.

Where is Rogue Intelligence Dog Training located?

We're based in Chandler, AZ, and serve the greater Phoenix Valley, including Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa, and Phoenix.

How long does Board & Train take?

It depends on the program: Basic Obedience runs 2–3 weeks, Public Access 4–5 weeks, and Advanced Obedience 6–8 weeks. Every dog progresses at its own pace within that structure.

What's the difference between Board & Train and Lessons?

Board & Train means your dog lives and trains with us daily in a structured environment, then we teach you how to maintain it at home. Lessons are one-on-one sessions where you and your dog train together from day one.

What training philosophy do you use?

Consistency, leadership, and understanding. We train for confidence, communication, and teamwork — not just memorized commands — so your dog can think, adapt, and stay focused through real-world distractions.

Do you train puppies and adult dogs?

Yes — our programs are built around your dog's individual needs, whether that's foundational work with a puppy or addressing established behaviors in an adult dog.

How much does dog training cost?

Investment depends on program format, length, goals, and support. Current published prices are loaded directly into the Board & Train and Lessons sections above so the amount you see matches our active catalog.

Are you a veteran-owned business?

Yes. Rogue Intelligence Dog Training is proudly veteran-owned and operated. The same discipline, structure, and follow-through we lived by carry into how we train dogs and coach their handlers.

Do you train reactive, anxious, or aggressive dogs?

Yes. Our programs are built around each dog's individual needs, including leash reactivity, anxiety, and behavior challenges. We focus on building confidence, impulse control, and clear communication so your dog can stay calm and focused through real-world distractions.

Do you offer e-collar training?

Yes, e-collar training is available as an option on select programs. We introduce tools fairly and clearly, always paired with foundation obedience and handler education — never as a shortcut around understanding.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Chandler, AZ, and train dogs throughout the East Valley and greater Phoenix metro — including Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa, Queen Creek, Ahwatukee, Scottsdale, Sun Lakes, and Phoenix.

How do progress updates work during Board & Train?

During Board & Train, your dog trains 5–6 days a week and we send video progress updates so you can watch the transformation happen. Every program finishes with hands-on owner training so you can maintain the results at home.

What is the Assistant Trainer Program?

The Assistant Trainer Program gives you the exact same Basic or Advanced Obedience curriculum, delivered by one of our Assistant Trainers under Thomas's direct supervision — every session plan and progress check is reviewed by the lead trainer. Same daily structure, same finished dog, at a more accessible investment.
