Dog Training Questions, Answered

Straight answers to the questions Phoenix Valley owners actually ask — no hedging, no sales pitch.

See Programs & Pricing Back to Guides

Is e-collar training safe for puppies? Does Board & Train ruin the bond with your dog? What breeds do you actually train? Below are direct answers to the questions we hear most from Chandler, Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa, and Queen Creek dog owners before they book.

Is e-collar training safe for puppies?

E-collar training is available as an option on select programs, but it's never used as a shortcut. We introduce tools fairly and clearly, always paired with foundation obedience and handler education first, so the dog understands the behavior before any tool is added.

What age should I start training my puppy?

Foundational work can start as soon as a puppy comes home — structure, boundaries, crate habits, and basic engagement don't need to wait for a certain age. Our programs are built around each dog's individual needs, whether that's early puppy foundation or work with an established adult dog.

Does Board & Train ruin the bond with my dog, or will my dog forget me?

No. During Board & Train your dog trains 5–6 days a week and we send video progress updates so you can watch the transformation happen. Every program finishes with hands-on owner training so the structure transfers to you, not just the dog — the bond is built through clear communication, not proximity alone.

What's the difference between Board & Train and private lessons?

Board & Train means your dog lives and trains with us daily in a structured environment, then we teach you how to maintain it at home. Lessons are one-on-one sessions where you and your dog train together from day one. Full comparison: Board & Train vs. Owner Lessons.

Do you train reactive, anxious, or aggressive dogs?

Yes. Our programs are built around each dog's individual needs, including leash reactivity, anxiety, and behavior challenges. We focus on building confidence, impulse control, and clear communication so the dog can stay calm and focused through real-world distractions.

Do you offer group classes?

No — every program is Board & Train or one-on-one private lessons. We don't run group classes, because the work that actually changes behavior is individualized to the dog and handler, not delivered in a group format.

What happens after training ends? Is there ongoing support?

Every program includes a 30-day follow-up period. Beyond that, booster lessons are available if you hit a plateau or want to add advanced behaviors like distance commands or off-leash work, and clients get access to the RI Dog Training network — resources, tips, and a community of handlers doing the same work.

What breeds do you train?

All of them. We work with puppies and adult dogs of every breed — from foundation obedience and leash manners to off-leash reliability and reactive-dog work.

How much does dog training cost in Chandler, AZ?

Investment depends on program format, length, goals, and support level — current published pricing is loaded directly into the Board & Train and Lessons sections on the homepage so what you see matches our active catalog. Full pricing breakdown: How Much Does Dog Training Cost in Arizona?

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Chandler, AZ, and train dogs throughout the East Valley and greater Phoenix metro — including Gilbert, Tempe, Mesa, Queen Creek, Ahwatukee, Scottsdale, Sun Lakes, and Phoenix.

Is Rogue Intelligence Dog Training veteran-owned?

Yes. Rogue Intelligence Dog Training is proudly veteran-owned and operated. The same discipline, structure, and follow-through carry into how dogs are trained and handlers are coached.

Is my dog a candidate for service dog training?

It depends on temperament, nerve strength, and trainability under real-world distraction — true public-access work is an extreme standard, not every dog is a fit. Reach out and describe your dog's background and we'll give you a straight answer on candidacy before committing to a program.
